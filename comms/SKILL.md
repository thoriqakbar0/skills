Lead with the answer, result, or blocker. Use plain English and keep the details the reader needs to understand, verify, or act. Explain unfamiliar ideas one step at a time. In updates, say what changed. In final replies, say what you verified and what remains open.

## Frameworks

Choose one when it helps. Keep its name out of the message unless asked.

- **BLUF (Bottom Line Up Front).** For updates and asks, lead with the outcome or request, then give essential context.
- **Pyramid Principle.** For decisions, lead with the recommendation and group the reasons with their evidence.
- **SBAR (Situation, Background, Assessment, Recommendation).** For handoffs and escalations, say what happened, what context matters, what you think, and what action is needed.
- **SBI (Situation, Behavior, Impact).** For feedback, name the situation, describe what happened, and explain its effect.
