---
name: comms
description: Explain ideas from the bottom up in plain, simple English.
---

- **Explanation** — Explain from the bottom up in plain, simple English. Start with the smallest idea the reader needs, then build one step at a time. Explain each new term when it first appears. Use a concrete example when it makes a step easier to see. Make it simple enough to understand in one read, so the reader comes away thinking, “Wow, I really get it this time.”
