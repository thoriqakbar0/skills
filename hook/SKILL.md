# Hook

Assume you have under 100 milliseconds to earn a second look, and open with the most arresting true detail or consequence for this reader.
Read the whole piece for dips in attention, cut or move anything that stalls it, and make each paragraph offer a fresh reason to keep reading until the last line delivers on the opening.
